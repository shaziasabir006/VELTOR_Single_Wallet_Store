# VELTOR — single-product storefront

Open index.html in a browser. One product is available: VELTOR Signature Leather Bifold (PKR 4,990). The existing hero, design, shopping cart, product details, reviews, FAQ and checkout remain. The product uses a branded WebP concept photo with a metallic V emblem.

The 20% discount still applies when buying two units of this wallet. The 10% future gift-code flow remains.

IMPORTANT: The checkout is a browser-local DEMO. Card payment is disabled pending a hosted PCI-compliant payment gateway, merchant onboarding and server-side payment confirmation. Cash on delivery is also a demo and does not transmit a real order. Customer details and orders remain in the current browser; no merchant dashboard or email fulfillment is connected. Do not enter real payment card details.

For live payments, integrate a verified Pakistan merchant gateway, secure server and webhook validation, inventory and fulfillment. Product photography is a concept render, not verified inventory photography.
